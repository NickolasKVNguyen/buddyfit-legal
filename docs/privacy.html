Privacy Policy

Buddy Fit is a fitness challenge app for iPhone and Apple Watch. This page explains, in plain words, what the app keeps and what it sends to a server.

Last updated: November 10, 2026

The short version: no ads, no tracking, no data sold. Your workouts and social details are stored so your friends and groups can see the progress you choose to share. You can delete your account in Settings.

Who we are

Buddy Fit is made by BuddyFit. For anything in this policy, contact [contact email].

What stays on your phone

Most of the app works on the device. These stay local unless you sign in and the item is part of your synced account listed below:

  • Your goals, units, theme and other preferences.
  • Workout drafts and workout history files.
  • Water and daily activity entries.
  • Reminder and notification settings.
  • GPS routes recorded during outdoor workouts. These route files stay on the device and on your paired Apple Watch; they are not uploaded to our server.
  • Crash reports that iOS hands to the app. They stay on the phone until you choose to send them.

Apple Health

With your permission, Buddy Fit reads steps, active energy, exercise minutes, stand hours and workouts from Apple Health so it can track your goals. When you finish a workout in the app, it writes that workout to Apple Health.

Health readings are used for your own goals and summaries only. They are never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared with third parties for their own purposes. You can change Health access at any time in the Health app or in iOS Settings.

What goes to our server when you sign in

Signing in is optional. When you sign in with Apple, we store the following on our server (hosted with Supabase) so that features like groups, leaderboards and friends work across devices:

  • Account: the email address Apple shares during sign-in (or Apple's private relay address if you choose Hide My Email), plus an account ID.
  • Profile: your display name, username and chosen avatar.
  • Fitness activity: the workouts you log and your goal progress — the totals your friends and group members see on leaderboards and challenge screens.
  • Social: your friend connections, group memberships and group events.
  • Content you create: posts and comments, including any photo you attach.
  • Safety records: reports you file and the list of people you block, so that blocking works on every device you use.
  • Push token: a device token from Apple, used only to send you notifications such as a friend starting a workout. You can turn these off in Settings.

We do not use any advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs, and the app does not track you across other apps or websites.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent your data.
  • We do not show ads or share data with advertisers or data brokers.
  • We do not read your Health data for marketing, insurance or research.
  • We do not post anything for you; posts and comments are only what you write.

Who can see what

  • Your profile, shared activity summaries, posts and comments can be seen by friends and by members of groups you join, depending on your sharing preferences in Settings.
  • Reports go to the person who moderates the app, so that reported content can be reviewed.
  • Your block list is used to hide their content and keep them from finding you in the app.

How long we keep things

Account data, workouts, posts and social records are kept while your account exists. When you delete your account, the server-side records tied to it are removed through our account-deletion routine. Copies in our hosting provider's backups are cleared within 30 days.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account yourself, inside the app: open Settings → Data & about and use Delete online account. Deleting removes your online profile and group membership and signs you out; workout history that only lives on your phone stays until you clear it with Clear Workout History or delete the app.

If you would rather ask us to do it, email [contact email].

Your choices

  • Share less: change what friends see in Settings, or turn on private training so no green dot appears.
  • Turn off notifications and reminders in Settings or in iOS Settings.
  • Change or revoke Apple Health access in the Health app.
  • Delete your account in Settings.

Children

Buddy Fit is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in an important way, we will update this page and change the date above. Continuing to use the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
